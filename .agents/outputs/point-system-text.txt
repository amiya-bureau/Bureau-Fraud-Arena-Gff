Leaderboard & Point System 
 
Overview 
The Fraud Arena runs three independent games. Each game has its own leaderboard (Today and All Days) plus a 
Combined view that sums a player's best score across all three. Points are recorded at the end of each run - the 
highest single-run score per game per player is counted.

Scoring Rules 
●​
Correct answer → full points for that level. 
●​
Wrong answer → run ends immediately (0 pts for that level). All previously banked points are recorded. 
●​
Timeout → run ends immediately. All previously banked points are recorded. 
●​
Near-Miss (multi-select only) → exactly one wrong selection in the group. Earns half the level's points 
and ends the run.

●​
Skip → available at levels. Skipping banks 0 pts for that level and advances to the next. 
 
 
2. Spoof the System 
Players attempt to fool Bureau's synthetic-image detector across three levels of increasing detector sensitivity. 
Points are earned by beating each level. Players can bank their points and walk away after Level 1 or 2, or risk 
continuing for a higher score.

3. Fraud Detective 
Players investigate five financial crime graph cases and identify the fraudulent entities (mule chains, rings, 
self-dealing networks). After the five cases, a bonus trivia round awards additional points.

4. Leaderboard Views 
The leaderboard is accessible from the main Arena screen. It refreshes every 10 seconds automatically. 
 
Scope Filters 
●​
Today - scores recorded during the current calendar day at the booth. 
●​
All Days - cumulative scores across the entire event. 
 
Game Filters 
●​
All - Combined score (sum of best runs across all three games per player). 
●​
Spot - Spot the Fraud only. 
●​
Spoof - Spoof the System only. 
●​
Detect - Fraud Detective only. 
 
Display 
●​
Top 10 players shown per view. 
●​
A player's own row is pinned at the bottom if they fall outside the top 10. 
●​
Rank 1 is highlighted in violet. Top 3 scores are shown in violet numerals.
